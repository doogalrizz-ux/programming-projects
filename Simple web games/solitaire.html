❓ How to Play

Goal: Move all cards to the 4 foundations (♠♥♦♣), Ace to King by suit.

Tableau: Stack cards in alternating colors, descending rank. Only Kings go on empty columns.

Stock: Click to deal to waste. Click empty stock to redeal.

Double-click any card to auto-send it to a foundation.
