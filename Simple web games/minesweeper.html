❓ How to Play

Left-click a cell to reveal it.
Right-click to place a 🚩 flag on a suspected mine.
Right-click again to place a ? mark, then again to clear.

Numbers show how many mines touch that cell.
Flag all mines without triggering any to win!

Difficulties:
Beginner: 9×9 with 10 mines
Intermediate: 16×16 with 40 mines
Expert: 30×16 with 99 mines
